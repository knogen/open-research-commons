From v0.3, original documentation, constitution drafts, research papers, proof files, and other original materials that this project has the right to license are offered under the **MIT License**, together with the website source code. This adopts [Issue #4](https://github.com/knogen/open-research-commons/issues/4). The complete, unmodified MIT license text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Scope and attribution

- Each submission must identify its actual copyright holder(s), the files or portions covered by MIT, and any excluded material. The project's collective copyright notice does not replace the copyright notices of individual works or transfer their ownership to the community.
- Copies or substantial portions of MIT-covered material must retain the copyright and permission notices required by MIT. The community also preserves each work's authors, version, and citation information as scholarly metadata; this practice does not add a new condition to the standard MIT license.
- Legal texts, policies, screenshots, logos, quotations, datasets, model assets, dependencies, and other third-party materials linked or included here remain subject to their own rights and terms. This notice does not relicense them under MIT. Contributors must identify their sources and applicable licenses and have permission or another applicable legal basis to make them public.
- The notice grants only rights the relevant copyright holders can grant. It does not create copyright in public-domain facts or otherwise unprotected material, or resolve third-party rights, privacy, patent, trademark, confidentiality, or separate database-rights questions.

## Earlier versions

The historical v0.1/v0.2 documents and their existing **CC BY 4.0** grants are preserved. Moving current original materials to MIT does not revoke previously granted CC BY rights for recipients who comply with that license. An earlier work may also be offered under MIT only by someone entitled to grant that additional permission; merely receiving it under CC BY does not remove its existing attribution and license requirements.

Historical source snapshots are reference records. Their third-party excerpts remain governed by the original materials' rights and terms, regardless of the version of this notice.
